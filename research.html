Research & Evaluation

Scientific characterization of PALF-based material

Material Evaluation

Mechanical Evaluation

The mechanical performance of the developed PALF-based material will be evaluated through tensile strength and elongation tests. Tensile strength will determine the ability of the material to withstand an applied force before breaking, while elongation at break will indicate its flexibility and stretchability. The results from the 0.8 mm and 2.0 mm thickness samples will be compared to determine the effect of thickness on the mechanical performance of the material.

Physical and Water Resistance Evaluation

Physical properties such as thickness, surface appearance and flexibility will be examined to ensure that the produced sheets have consistent characteristics. A water absorption test will also be conducted to determine the material's ability to resist moisture. This is important because excessive water absorption may affect the strength, durability and appearance of the leather-alternative material during its potential applications.

Chemical and Morphological Evaluation

Chemical and morphological characteristics can be evaluated using Fourier Transform Infrared Spectroscopy (FTIR) and Scanning Electron Microscopy (SEM). FTIR can be used to identify the functional groups present in PALF and observe possible chemical changes after NaOH treatment. SEM can provide information about the fiber surface, distribution and interaction between PALF and the polymer matrix. These analyses can help determine whether the chemical treatment improves the bonding and overall structure of the developed material.

Biodegradability Evaluation

The environmental performance of the PALF-based material will also be evaluated through a biodegradability test. The samples can be exposed to controlled soil conditions for a selected period, after which changes in appearance, structure and weight can be observed. The degradation behavior will provide an indication of the material's potential to decompose naturally and support its application as a more environmentally friendly alternative to conventional synthetic leather.

Overall Evaluation

The results obtained from these evaluations will be compared between the different thicknesses to identify the most suitable material characteristics for the intended applications. The evaluation will help determine whether the developed PALF-based material provides a suitable balance of strength, flexibility, water resistance, durability and biodegradability for non-structural leather-alternative products.
